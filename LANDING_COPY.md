## FAQs

### 1. Is the audit really free?

Yes. The basic audit is completely free, forever. No sign-up, no email, no credit card. We offer a paid tier ($49/mo) for teams that want quarterly re-audits, PDF reports, and ongoing monitoring — but the one-time audit is free.

### 2. How do you know the pricing data is accurate?

We manually verify pricing for every tool against the vendor's official pricing page. All prices include source URLs and verification dates (see our [PRICING_DATA.md](PRICING_DATA.md)). We re-verify quarterly and whenever a vendor announces changes.

### 3. Do you store my data?

We store your audit results so you can share the results URL with your team. We don't store any personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily enter your email to receive a PDF report. We never sell data. See our privacy policy for details.

### 4. What if the audit says I'm already optimized?

Great — that's a valid (and valuable) outcome. You'll get a low overspend score and confirmation that your stack is well-configured. You can re-run the audit anytime your tools or team size change.

### 5. Can I audit tools that aren't listed?

We currently support 9 AI tools and APIs (Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Windsurf, OpenAI API, Anthropic API, Gemini API). If your tool isn't listed, let us know — we're adding new tools every month based on user requests.
