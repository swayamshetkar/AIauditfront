## Interview Protocol

### Before the Interview

- [ ] Confirm the interviewee manages or influences AI tool purchasing
- [ ] Prepare their company context (size, stage, industry)
- [ ] Test recording setup

### Questions (ask all 3 interviewees the same questions)

1. **Context**: What AI tools does your team currently use? How many people are on the team?
2. **Buying Process**: How did you decide which AI tools to adopt? Who made the decision?
3. **Visibility**: Do you know how much your team spends on AI tools per month? (exact number or estimate)
4. **Pain**: What frustrates you most about managing AI tool subscriptions?
5. **Overlap**: Do you think any of your tools overlap in functionality? Have you tried to consolidate?
6. **Switching**: Have you ever downgraded or cancelled an AI tool? What triggered that?
7. **Value Prop Test**: If a tool could audit your AI stack and tell you exactly where you're overspending, would you use it? Would you pay for it?
8. **Pricing Sensitivity**: What would you expect to pay for that? What would be too expensive?
9. **Distribution**: Where do you go to learn about new developer tools? (newsletters, communities, peers)
10. **Closing**: Is there anything about AI tool spending that I should have asked but didn't?
