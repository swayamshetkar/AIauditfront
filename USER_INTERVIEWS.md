## Interview Protocol

### Before the Interview

- [ ] Confirm the interviewee manages or influences AI tool purchasing
- [ ] Prepare their company context (size, stage, industry)
- [ ] Test recording setup

### Questions (ask all 3 interviewees the same questions)

1. **Context**: What AI tools does your team currently use? How many people are on the team?
2. **Buying Process**: How did you decide which AI tools to adopt? Who made the decision?
3. **Visibility**: Do you know how much your team spends on AI tools per month? (exact number or estimate)
4. **Pain**: What frustrates you most about managing AI tool subscriptions?
5. **Overlap**: Do you think any of your tools overlap in functionality? Have you tried to consolidate?
6. **Switching**: Have you ever downgraded or cancelled an AI tool? What triggered that?
7. **Value Prop Test**: If a tool could audit your AI stack and tell you exactly where you're overspending, would you use it? Would you pay for it?
8. **Pricing Sensitivity**: What would you expect to pay for that? What would be too expensive?
9. **Distribution**: Where do you go to learn about new developer tools? (newsletters, communities, peers)
10. **Closing**: Is there anything about AI tool spending that I should have asked but didn't?

| **Name** | Vinyas |

| **Company** | Early-stage SaaS startup (B2B) |
| **Company Size** | 8 engineers, 14 total |
| **Stage** | Seed / Pre-Series A |
| **Date** | 2026-05-22 |
| **Duration** | ~25 minutes (async chat + call) |

### Key Quotes

- "We just kept adding tools — someone tried Cursor, someone else was already on Copilot, and then half the team was also paying for ChatGPT Plus out of pocket. Nobody ever stopped to check if we needed all three."
- "I genuinely have no idea what our total AI spend is. I know my card gets charged, but I'd have to go dig through five different billing portals to find out."
- "The overlap between Cursor and Copilot is insane. They literally do the same thing in the IDE. But people have preferences, so nobody wants to give theirs up."
- "If something just told me 'hey, you're burning $800/mo on duplicate tools and here's how to fix it,' I'd use that today."

### AI Tools Used

| Tool | Plan | Monthly Spend | Seats |
|------|------|---------------|-------|
| Cursor | Pro | $160/mo | 8 |
| GitHub Copilot | Business | $152/mo | 8 |
| ChatGPT | Plus | $100/mo | 5 |
| Claude | Pro | $40/mo | 2 |
| OpenAI API | Pay As You Go | ~$90/mo | — |

**Total estimated spend: ~$542/mo ($6,504/yr)**

### Pain Points

1. **No visibility** — Vinyas had no single dashboard showing total AI spend across the team. Each tool bills separately and nobody aggregates it.
2. **Tool overlap** — Both Cursor and Copilot are running simultaneously in the same IDEs for 8 engineers. Vinyas acknowledged they do the same thing but said "people are attached to their workflows."
3. **Shadow AI spending** — Some engineers expense ChatGPT Plus personally and get reimbursed. It doesn't show up on any team billing, making it invisible to leadership.
4. **No framework for evaluating** — When someone asks "should we try Windsurf?", Vinyas has no process to evaluate whether it adds value or just adds cost.

### Reaction to Spend Node Value Prop

- **Would he use it?** — "Absolutely. I'd run it right now if you gave me the link."
- **Would he pay for it?** — "If it saves me $200+/mo, I'd pay $20-30/mo for it easily. The ROI is obvious."
- **What convinced him** — The idea that it shows a single efficiency score and exact dollar savings without requiring him to manually audit five billing portals.
- **Concern** — "Would it work if I don't know the exact spend? Sometimes I'm guessing the monthly number."

### Surprises

- Vinyas didn't realize Cursor and Copilot were redundant until I specifically asked about overlap. He said "I knew it in the back of my head, but nobody ever put a dollar amount on it. Seeing it as $152/mo wasted — that actually makes me want to fix it."
- He was more interested in the "adoption deficit" angle than expected — asked if the tool could also tell him if his team was *under*-investing in AI compared to competitors.

### Follow-Up Actions

- [x] Sent Vinyas the live link to test the audit flow
- [x] He ran the audit with his real stack — confirmed the redundancy detection flagged Cursor + Copilot correctly
- [ ] Follow up in 2 weeks to see if he actually consolidated
